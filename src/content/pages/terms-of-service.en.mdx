## Camera, Recording, and Audio

You choose whether to grant camera and microphone access. The browser requests camera access when the Gesture Synth homepage opens. If you allow it, a live preview appears while local hand tracking loads. Browser autoplay rules require one click or touch before generated synth audio starts. The microphone is requested only when you start an MP4 recording and is mixed locally with that generated audio. Gesture Synth captures only its own performance canvas, so it does not request screen-sharing access.
