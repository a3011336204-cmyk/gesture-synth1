## An online gesture synthesizer for music lovers

Gesture Synth is a free online gesture synthesizer that turns two-hand movements into musical control. It is designed for music lovers who want to play chords, shape tone, and record a short performance without installing software or creating an account.

The instrument runs in a modern browser. Your camera provides the hand movement needed for playing, while the browser creates the synth sound locally with Web Audio.

## Start, allow the camera, then play

1. Open the instrument and select **Start playing**.
2. Allow camera access when your browser asks for it.
3. Keep both hands visible to play and shape the sound.

Gesture Synth asks for camera access only after you start the instrument. You can stop camera access by closing the page, revoking browser permission, or disabling the camera on your device.

## What your hands control

Your left hand selects the scale degree and major or minor mode. Your right hand controls chord voicing, octave, volume, and filter. Keeping both hands in view gives the instrument the information it needs to turn movement into stable musical states.

This is a performance tool, not a replacement for a physical keyboard or a complete DAW. It is built for quick exploration: make a chord, change its character with movement, and capture a short take when an idea appears.

## Browser and device requirements

For the best chance of a smooth session, use a current or previous major release of Chrome, Edge, or Safari on a device with a working camera. Firefox support is best effort. Browser support for camera access, Web Audio, canvas capture, hardware acceleration, and MP4 recording can vary by browser and device.

Ordinary playing needs camera permission. Recording additionally asks for microphone permission only when you choose to begin an MP4 recording. If your browser or device does not support one of those media features, the related part of the instrument may be unavailable.

## What happens to camera and recording data

Camera frames, hand landmarks, microphone audio, generated synth audio, and recordings are processed in your browser. Gesture Synth does not provide cloud media storage or upload performance recordings. When you record, the browser combines the live synth canvas with microphone and generated synth audio, then downloads the result as an MP4 on supported browsers. A recording stops automatically after five minutes.

## Technology behind the instrument

Gesture Synth uses [MediaPipe Hand Landmarker for the web](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker/web_js) to detect hand landmarks on the device. Sound is generated with the browser's [Web Audio API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API). Camera permission follows the browser's [getUserMedia](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaDevices/getUserMedia) model, and recording relies on browser media capabilities such as [MediaRecorder](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaRecorder).

These references describe the underlying browser and hand-tracking technologies. They do not guarantee that every browser, camera, microphone, or device will behave identically with Gesture Synth.

## Make and share a short performance

Start with both hands clearly framed, use the left hand to choose harmony, then use the right hand to change the chord's voicing, octave, volume, and filter. When you are ready to capture an idea, start recording and grant microphone access only if you want microphone audio mixed into the take. The downloaded MP4 remains under your control; make sure you have the rights to publish anything you record.
