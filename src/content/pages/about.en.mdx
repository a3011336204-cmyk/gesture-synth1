## Independent browser music tool

Gesture Synth is created and maintained by Cian as a free browser instrument for music lovers. It turns two-hand gestures into harmony, voicing, octave, volume, and filter controls, then lets you record a local performance when your browser supports it.

The product is designed around a simple boundary: camera frames, hand landmarks, microphone audio, synth audio, and recordings stay on your device. Gesture Synth does not require an account or upload a performance to a cloud library.
