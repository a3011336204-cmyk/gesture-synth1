## Get in touch

Gesture Synth is a free online gesture synthesizer for music lovers. For questions about playing, camera permission, recording, privacy, or feedback, email [a3011336204@gmail.com](mailto:a3011336204@gmail.com).

## Include the useful details

To make a technical question easier to investigate, include:

- Your browser and version.
- Your device type and operating system.
- Whether you were starting the instrument, granting camera access, or recording.
- The exact error message or a short description of what happened.

Do not send recordings, camera footage, or other personal media unless it is necessary for your request and you are comfortable sharing it.
